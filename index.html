Submissions Portal

Submit

Review the guidelines below before submitting your manuscript to GUJIP for consideration.

Submit manuscripts by email to gujip@georgetown.edu.

Eligibility and General Submission Rules

  1. Any undergraduate or recent graduate (within one year of their graduation date) in any field may submit an original article to be considered for publication in GUJIP. No submissions by high school students or research conducted while being in high school will be considered for review.
  2. The author has the right to publish the work. Authors should take care to secure copyright for figures that they did not create.
  3. Submitted articles cannot have been previously published, nor be forthcoming in an archival journal or book (print or electronic). Please note: "publication" in a working-paper series does not constitute prior publication. Presenting your research in a poster or oral presentation is not recognized as publication of your research.
  4. By submitting a manuscript to GUJIP, the author stipulates that the material is not currently under review at another journal (electronic or print) and that the author will not submit the material to another journal (electronic or print) until the completion of the editorial decision process at GUJIP. Authors are permitted to post their articles on open access preprint repositories if they so choose.

If you have concerns about the submission terms for GUJIP, please contact the editorial board at gujip@georgetown.edu.

Formatting Requirements

GUJIP requires articles to be formatted upon initial submission according to the following criteria:

  1. All articles must be written in English.
  2. Submit the manuscript formatted in 12 pt. double-spaced with Times New Roman font. We require all submissions use Chicago Style, Notes and Bibliography (18th Edition) citations. Submitted manuscripts not in Chicago Style, Notes and Bibliography (18th Edition) format or lacking citations in Chicago Style, Notes and Bibliography (18th Edition) will be rejected during pre-screening and will not be reviewed.
  3. The article is a high-quality PDF file, or a Microsoft Word file that can be converted to a PDF file. We suggest that you review your article through our submissions portal page to ensure that it has been received in the correct format. Scratched-out, blacked-out, password-protected, and blank articles will not be reviewed.
  4. All images and graphs are of high quality resolution (300 dpi).
  5. The author must have rights to use all figures and images. If there is a cost associated with publishing any images, the journal will not cover this cost.
  6. Each paper should be submitted in Google Docs format.
  7. Academic papers should be approximately 8,000 words. Features and essays should be approximately 1,500 words.
  8. Authors are encouraged to submit any photography, art, or graphics pertaining to their pieces.
  9. Please submit a title page containing the title of your piece, your full name, school, department, year of graduation, and contact information.
  10. Examples of the types of papers that have been submitted in the past include past or current research projects, relevant classroom papers, and senior theses.

AI Statement

GUJIP reserves the right to reject any papers that we suspect of generative AI usage in the production of the paper, including, but not limited to, large language models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Deepseek. Examples include, but are not limited to: proofreading manuscripts, creating a framework or outline for writing, preparing a bibliography, and multimedia generation. Manuscripts that have been submitted with non-generative AI must have proper documentation (e.g., prompts, model, and version used) in their methodology and produce replicable results.

About Us

The Georgetown Undergraduate Journal of International Politics (GUJIP) is a peer-reviewed, open-access, student-run journal dedicated to publishing rigorous undergraduate research in international affairs. Founded by students in Georgetown's School of Foreign Service, GUJIP fills a critical gap in the undergraduate research pipeline by providing a formal venue for serious scholarly work in international politics.

Affiliated with the Mortara Center for International Studies, GUJIP is committed to multilingual, cross-cultural, and interdisciplinary scholarship. The Journal works closely with faculty reviewers to guide students through the full research and publication process, supporting early-career scholars and strengthening Georgetown's undergraduate research community.
